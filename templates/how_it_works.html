How It Works

Finding or reporting a lost item on campus is simple.

📝
STEP 1

Report an Item

🔍
STEP 2

Search for Items

Browse lost and found reports and use the search and filter options to find the item you're looking for.

🤝
STEP 3

Find & Reunite

Open the item details, check the information and help return the lost belonging to its owner.
